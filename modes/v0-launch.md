# Mode: v0-launch -- Idea To First Real Customer

Evaluate whether a project, founder need, business idea, or outbound target can be moved from ambiguity to a first real customer through a bounded paid sprint.

This mode is a high-upside lane, not the default first pursuit. Use it when there is visible buyer intent and a plausible path to paid scoping with first-build work or a sprint quickly. If the path to payment is weak, route back to `opportunity` and rank the lead lower than simpler paid remote work.

## Definition

A first real customer is someone outside the founder's inner circle who takes a concrete market action:

- pays
- books
- signs
- commits to a pilot
- submits a qualified lead
- uses the product in a real workflow

Interest, compliments, demo views, or friend feedback do not count.

## Evaluation Questions

1. Who is the buyer or user?
2. What market action counts as the first real customer?
3. What is the smallest v0 that can create that action?
4. What must be built, automated, written, researched, or coordinated?
5. What can be delivered remotely?
6. What domain facts must the client provide?
7. What can agents accelerate?
8. What validation path catches model or domain errors?
9. What blocks first-customer progress within 2-4 weeks?
10. What should be sold first: paid scoping with first-build work, sprint, or smaller task?
11. How quickly could this become paid work?

## Disqualification Signals

Do not hard-reject on the first pass, but flag:

- no reachable buyer or channel
- no concrete first-customer action
- regulated approval needed before any customer test
- hardware/field operations where remote execution is the blocker
- marketplace liquidity required before any useful validation
- client cannot provide fast domain access or decisions
- vague AI wrapper with no specific buyer pain
- unpaid/equity-only scope presented as normal paid work

## Commercial Bias

Prefer paid scoping that includes the first useful build artifact, a small fixed deliverable, or a 2-4 week sprint. Avoid unbounded "build my whole SaaS" framing. The offer is to clarify, build, launch/test, document, and hand over enough for the client to reach a first real customer signal.
